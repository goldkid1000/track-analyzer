How live coaching works

Coaching on this device — no cloud, no sign-in, no video leaves your device. The camera watches your rep, names what it sees, and gives one fix at a time.

⏱️ Meal timing

2–3 hours before

Full meal: carbs + protein, low fat, low fiber. Example: rice + grilled chicken + banana.

30–60 min before

Light carbs only — a banana, granola bar, or toast with jam. Nothing heavy.

Within 2 hours after

Protein + carbs to recover. The classic: chocolate milk + banana, or eggs + oatmeal.

Morning

Protein-rich breakfast within an hour of waking — eggs, Greek yogurt, or oatmeal with peanut butter.

Through the day

3–4 balanced meals, don't skip. Keep protein high for recovery; carbs can run lighter than training days.

Before bed

Slow-digesting protein (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, or milk) to feed muscles overnight.

Skip greasy food and big fiber loads right before sprinting — both slow you down and upset the stomach.

🍽️ Dorm-friendly ideas

Dining hall

  • Grilled chicken + rice + vegetables
  • Eggs + oatmeal + fruit (breakfast)
  • Pasta + meat sauce + side salad

Microwave

  • Oatmeal packet + peanut butter + banana
  • Rice cup + canned chicken or tuna
  • Quesadilla: tortilla + cheese + beans

No-cook

  • Greek yogurt + granola + berries
  • PB sandwich + chocolate milk
  • Cottage cheese + fruit + trail mix
